## United States: Non-Contiguous Destinations

The U.S. rates and estimates above apply to the contiguous United States. Standard shipping to Alaska, Hawaii and supported U.S. territories, freely associated states and military addresses is US$6. Express is temporarily unavailable for these addresses. Eligibility and destination-specific estimates are confirmed at checkout.



## Delivery Estimates

Transit times are estimates after dispatch, not guaranteed arrival dates. Carrier delays, customs processing, weather, local holidays and remote-area delivery can affect arrival. We will help investigate delayed or missing shipments; this does not affect your statutory rights.



## Tracking

We’ll email your tracking number as soon as your order ships. Please allow 24–72 hours for tracking to update.



## Duties and Import Charges

Our selected shipping services use duties-paid (DDP) arrangements. Applicable import charges covered by the service are handled by us or our logistics partners; this does not mean the goods are legally tax-exempt. If a carrier asks you for an unexpected import payment, contact us before paying so we can check it.



## Order Cancellations

You may request cancellation of unfulfilled items in your order at any time before those items are fulfilled. Submit your request through your customer account, where available, or email [support@ooraliving.com](mailto:support@ooraliving.com) with your order number.


For partially fulfilled orders, this cancellation process applies only to the unfulfilled items. Once an item has been fulfilled, please follow our Return & Refund Policy. This does not affect your statutory withdrawal or other mandatory consumer rights.

## Address Changes, Undeliverable or Missing Orders

Please check your delivery address before placing an order. If you need to correct it, email us as soon as possible. We cannot guarantee changes once an order has been dispatched. For a delivery problem or missing parcel, contact us with your order number so we can help.



## Returns and Refunds

Please see our [Return & Refund Policy](/policies/refund-policy) for return windows, statutory withdrawal rights and return shipping costs. If you have questions, email [support@ooraliving.com](mailto:support@ooraliving.com).
