Order Cancellations

You may request cancellation of unfulfilled items in your order at any time before those items are fulfilled. Submit your request through your customer account, where available, or email support@ooraliving.com with your order number.

For partially fulfilled orders, this cancellation process applies only to the unfulfilled items. Once an item has been fulfilled, please follow our Return & Refund Policy. This does not affect your statutory withdrawal or other mandatory consumer rights.

Return Window

You may request a return within 7 calendar days of receiving your order.

For our voluntary 7-day return policy, the item must:

  • Be unused and in its original condition.
  • Include all original packaging, accessories, manuals, and inserts.
  • Be free from signs of misuse, damage, or unauthorized modification.
  • Be accompanied by proof of purchase (order number or receipt).

Our voluntary return window is 7 calendar days from delivery, except where the statutory withdrawal period below or other mandatory consumer rights apply.

Customers in the European Union, the United Kingdom and Norway have 14 calendar days after receiving the last item in their order to notify us that they wish to withdraw from an online purchase, without giving a reason. You must then send the goods back within 14 calendar days after notifying us. Where applicable, a deadline falling on a weekend or public holiday extends to the next business day.

For statutory withdrawal, you may inspect the product as you would in a shop. Unopened packaging and an entirely unused product are not conditions for exercising this right. Please return the product with its accessories and packaging where possible. We may make a lawful deduction only for diminished value caused by handling beyond what is necessary to establish the product’s nature, characteristics and functioning.

These periods do not limit your statutory rights for faulty, damaged, incorrect or misdescribed products. Any more protective mandatory local consumer rights continue to apply.

Compatibility Disclaimer

TapNest is designed to work with iPhones. However, NFC performance and magnetic functionality may vary depending on factors including, but not limited to:

  • iPhone model
  • Operating system version
  • NFC settings and permissions
  • Phone case thickness or material
  • Magnetic accessories or metal attachments
  • Third-party applications and software limitations

Performance differences resulting from these factors are considered compatibility limitations, not manufacturing defects.

Issues arising solely from device compatibility—including but not limited to inconsistent NFC detection, slower response times, reduced magnetic performance, or limitations caused by device settings or accessories—do not constitute product defects or product quality issues.

Returns requested solely due to compatibility limitations will be handled as standard returns under this policy and are not considered warranty claims. This disclaimer does not exclude your statutory rights if the product is faulty, does not match its description or fails to meet applicable conformity requirements.

If you are unsure whether your device is compatible, we encourage you to contact us before placing your order.

Adhesive Removal

TapNest is designed to be securely mounted using high-strength 3M adhesive.

If you wish to remove the product, we recommend doing so carefully using appropriate methods and tools, such as:

  • Adhesive remover designed for 3M adhesive residues
  • Dental floss or fishing line to gently separate the adhesive from the mounting surface
  • A plastic scraper or plastic pry tool (avoid using metal tools whenever possible)

Please remove the product slowly and carefully to avoid damaging either the product or the mounting surface.

Do not pull the product directly away from the surface using excessive force, as this may damage the product.

Any damage caused by improper removal, excessive force, the use of inappropriate tools, or failure to follow the recommended removal methods is considered customer-caused damage and is not covered under oora’s warranty, return, or replacement policy.

If you have any questions about safely removing your TapNest, please contact support@ooraliving.com before attempting removal. We’re happy to help.

Refund Process

Once your returned item has been received and inspected, we will notify you of the outcome.

If approved:

  • Your refund will be issued to the original payment method.
  • Please allow 5–10 business days for the refund to appear, depending on your payment provider.

For voluntary change-of-mind returns, original shipping charges are non-refundable unless required by law. For statutory withdrawal from the whole order in the European Union, the United Kingdom or Norway, we will also refund the cost of our least expensive Standard delivery option paid for that order. Any additional charge for choosing Express over Standard is not refundable under the withdrawal right. If Standard delivery was free, there is no original Standard shipping charge to refund.

For statutory withdrawal, we will issue the refund without undue delay and within 14 calendar days of receiving your withdrawal notice. We may withhold it until we receive the goods back or you provide evidence of sending them back, whichever occurs first. Refunds use your original payment method without a refund fee. The 5–10 business days above describe payment-provider processing after a refund is issued and do not extend statutory refund deadlines.

Nothing in this policy excludes or limits your mandatory consumer rights.
