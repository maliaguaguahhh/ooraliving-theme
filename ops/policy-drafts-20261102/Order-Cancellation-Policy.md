Order Cancellations

You may request cancellation of unfulfilled items in your order at any time before those items are fulfilled. Submit your request through your customer account, where available, or email support@ooraliving.com with your order number.

For partially fulfilled orders, this cancellation process applies only to the unfulfilled items. Once an item has been fulfilled, please follow our Return & Refund Policy. This does not affect your statutory withdrawal or other mandatory consumer rights.
