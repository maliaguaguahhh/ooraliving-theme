Orders normally ship within 1 business day. Standard and Express charges and estimated transit times vary by destination; see the shipping options shown for your country and the final options at checkout. Transit estimates are business days after dispatch. Express is temporarily unavailable where no Express service is offered.


You may request a return within 7 calendar days of delivery under our voluntary return policy. Customers in the European Union, the United Kingdom and Norway have 14 calendar days from delivery to notify us of statutory withdrawal, followed by 14 calendar days to send the goods back. Conditions and return shipping costs are explained in our [Return & Refund Policy](/policies/refund-policy). Your statutory rights are not affected.
